# Incarnam weapon matrix: catalog

216 weapons: 18 races x 12 categories. 212 generated by the race x category kit and baked at 512 PBR; Iop's 4 bespoke weapons are in `export/<Name>/` at 2K.

Triangles per generated weapon: min 1542, max 7892, mean 4981. Every weapon is within its R6 length range (`blender/lib/kit/core.py` SIZES).

Files: `export/pack/<Race>/<Weapon>/` contains `<Weapon>.fbx` (main mesh, with the emissive accent as a separate `_Glow` mesh inside it: use Neon or your VFX), `_L.fbx` and `_L_Glow.fbx` (the mirrored left gauntlet or blade for fists and dual blades, sharing the right one's textures), `_Head.fbx` and `_ChainLink.fbx` (chain weapons; use the WarlinkChain runtime with the two `ChainEnd` attachments), `textures/` (Color/Normal/Roughness/Metalness) and `_stats.json`.

## Known limits

- **Fists** all share the same boxy cuff, because it has to fit the R6 1×1 arm block. Each race is told apart by trim, colour, knuckle spikes and plates rather than by overall shape. They are the weakest category.
- **Not checked in Roblox yet.** None of this has been imported into Studio. Check scale and orientation by importing one weapon first.
- **Textures are 512 per weapon.** That keeps a 212-weapon pack light. Any single weapon can be re-baked at 1K or 2K by changing `TEX` in `blender/build_matrix.py`.
- **Rebuilding:** `blender/build_matrix.py --race <r> --cat <c>` rebuilds one weapon; `blender/bake_matrix.sh` rebuilds them all and can resume where it stopped.
