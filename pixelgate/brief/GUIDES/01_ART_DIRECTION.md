# PIXELGATE — Visual art direction / factory baseline

## Reading the supplied five images
These user-supplied concepts show PREMIUM STYLIZED fantasy weapons, not the desired factory appearance of the guns. Transfer their craftsmanship: beautiful hero silhouettes, clear design hierarchy, bespoke carved/machined detail, strong material transitions, intentional accent placement, crisp edge treatment, layered planes, and painterly but clean readability.

Do NOT transfer their literal ornate blades, spikes, carved runes, butterflies, glowing crystal cores, baroque gold swirls, branded logos, or fantastical silhouettes to default firearms. Those belong to a completely separate potential future cosmetic-skin line. Do NOT copy or recreate the third-party designs/branding, including the stylized A logo, Farlands text and GG/ATTERACT markings; source screenshots are only private moodboard references.

## Factory design rule
- Modern competitive Roblox shooter: stylized rather than photographic or toy-like.
- Weapons must be identifiable first as AK-47/M4A1/SCAR/etc. Use plausible external profiles and clean material language.
- Big readable silhouette > medium mechanical bands > small surface details. Avoid surface-noise overload.
- Silhouettes and ergonomics matter from BOTH first-person camera angle and world third-person angle.
- Realistic-looking but simplified hard-surface articulation. Preserve visibly correct relative placement of magazine, bolt, slide, cylinder, string, feed box and rotation cluster.
- Highly resolved bevel highlights and purposeful edge accents; no random scratches all over.
- Use cohesive factory palette of black, graphite, steel, neutral tan, walnut, low-key olive; choose gun-appropriate combinations.
- Default weapons: zero magic VFX or ornate engraved skins. Allow only gameplay muzzle flashes, brief eject effects, barrel heat and gun-specific audio.
- Keep a clean mask system for future camos/skins: `Body`, `Furniture`, `MetalAccent`, `Magazine`, `Wear`, `Emissive_FutureSkinOnly` (empty/black in factory).
- No watermarks or text baked into final meshes unless unique original PIXELGATE markings approved by the user.

## Art review shots per weapon
A. Flat side-on beauty view on charcoal, B. front 3/4, C. player first-person idle, D. ADS if applicable, E. parts exploded just to SHOW named animation-ready pieces (not an assembly guide), F. short animated loop demonstrating reload, inspect and fire.
